For security improvements and domain structure unification within the AIS CR infrastructure, the AMČR website now runs under the domain <https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/>.  
The previous domains <http://www.archeologickamapa.cz/> and <http://www.amapa.cz/> remain fully functional and redirect to the new address, including subpaths.
