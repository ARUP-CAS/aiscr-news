---
title: Notification System Updates
excerpt: The notification processing system has been updated.
badge: Update
---

The system for processing notifications in the [online form for reporting construction or other interventions](http://www.archeologickamapa.cz/oznameni) has changed. In order to limit the issuance of confirmations with incorrect or incomplete information, confirmation of notifications is now sent to the specified email address only after the content has been checked by the Archaeological Institutes.
