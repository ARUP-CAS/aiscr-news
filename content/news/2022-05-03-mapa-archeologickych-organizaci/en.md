A new [Map of Archaeological Organizations](https://oao.aiscr.cz/) has been launched, providing an overview of all organizations authorized to conduct field archaeological research in the Czech Republic. The application contains the most up-to-date information on their territorial jurisdiction, both from an administrative point of view and in terms of the research carried out.
