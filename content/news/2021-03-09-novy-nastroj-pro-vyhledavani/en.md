---
title: New Search Tool
excerpt: Search modules are now available via the Digital Archive.
badge: Update
---

In connection with the improvement of applications and the gradual transition to a web environment, there has been an important change in the provision of search tools in the AMČR. The Map and Search modules are now available via the [AMCR Digital Archive](https://digiarchiv.aiscr.cz/), rather than via the original desktop application. The search function has been significantly improved and linked to other functions of the Digital Archive. We believe that you will appreciate this change and that it will help to make AMČR data more accessible for the widest possible use.
