A new issue of the AMCR Newsletter (No. 6) has been published.  
It provides information on publishing documents in the [AMCR Digital Archive](http://digiarchiv.amapa.cz/) under new licensing agreements and reports on large data sets newly imported into the system.  
The document is available in the [“Downloads”](https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/?page=documents) section.
