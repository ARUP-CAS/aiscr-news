We are delighted to announce that the new AMCR web application is now live. Here is an overview of the newly available addresses:

<https://amcr.aiscr.cz/> - AMCR web application
<https://amcr-help.aiscr.cz/> - user documentation
<https://amcr.aiscr.cz/oznameni> - form for reporting construction or other projects
<https://amcr-tr.aiscr.cz/> - version of the application intended for training and public testing

In the coming days, we will be working on related changes in the AMCR Digital Archive and OAI-PMH API, where you can expect possible outages or partially unavailable data. We will inform you here when both services are fully operational.
