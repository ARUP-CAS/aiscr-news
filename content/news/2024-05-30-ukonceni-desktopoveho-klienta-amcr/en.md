---
title: End of Desktop AMCR Client
excerpt: The desktop AMCR client will be discontinued on 13 July 2024.
badge: Update
---

From July 13, 2024, the AMCR desktop client will be permanently shut down and replaced by a new web version of the application. For two to three weeks, the AMCR desktop client and AMCR API will be unavailable, and the AMCR Digital Archive may also experience outages. At the same time, the accompanying websites, this AMCR home page and AMCR help page (<https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/help/>), and the form used to report construction or other projects carried out in areas with archaeological finds (<https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/oznameni>) will also be migrated to the new versions.

In connection with the transition to the new version of AMCR, two online training sessions primarily intended for OAO (archaeology) employees will take place on June 26 (<https://cesnet.zoom.us/j/96080975576>) and July 10 (<https://cesnet.zoom.us/j/98701161691>), both starting at 9:00 a.m. via the Zoom application.

Please submit notifications of construction or other projects carried out in areas with archaeological finds in advance by mid-July, or later in the new application. During the outage, please address urgent notifications directly to the archaeological heritage departments of the Archaeological Institutes in Prague (<https://www.arup.cas.cz/oznameni-o-stavebni-cinnosti-na-uzemi-s-archeologickymi-nalezy/>) and Brno (<https://www.arub.cz/archeologicke-sluzby/informace-pro-stavebniky/>). Confirmation of compliance with the reporting obligation will be sent for these cases after the launch of the new version of AMCR at the beginning of August. If you need confirmation earlier, it will be issued upon request.
