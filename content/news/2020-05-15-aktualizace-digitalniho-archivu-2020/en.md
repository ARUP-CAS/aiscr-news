The [Digital Archive](https://digiarchiv.aiscr.cz/) has been updated with a number of new features! These include the ability to download full versions of documents, improved preview generation, added user help, a new way of browsing, automatically generated citations, and many other minor fixes and improvements that will ensure greater user comfort. In addition, you will find two completely new modules on the main screen—the Record of Individual Findings and the 3D Library, which will soon begin to fill up with interesting data. And you too can contribute to them through the new AMČR web client!
