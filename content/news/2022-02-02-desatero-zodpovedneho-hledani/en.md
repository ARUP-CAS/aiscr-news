An information leaflet entitled ["Ten Commandments of Responsible Metal Detecting"](https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/downloads/Zodpovedne_hledani.pdf) has been added to the [AMCR-PAS website](https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/pas). It briefly summarizes the most important principles related to amateur archaeological searching.
