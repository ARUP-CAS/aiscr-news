---
title: Operation of IDAV Ended
excerpt: The Internet Database of Archaeological Fieldwork (IDAV) has been discontinued. Data are now accessible through the AMCR system.
badge: Update
---

As of 1 September 2017, the Internet Database of Archaeological Fieldwork (IDAV) has been permanently discontinued.  
To browse the data, use the AMCR system.  
A converter for identifiers between the two systems is available [here](https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/downloads/IDAV_prevodnik_AMCR.xls).
