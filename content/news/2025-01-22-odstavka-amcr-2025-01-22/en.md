---
title: AMCR Outage
excerpt: A planned AMCR outage will take place on 22 January 2025.
badge: Outage
---

On Wednesday, January 22, 2025, from 6:00 p.m., there will be a planned shutdown of AMCR due to the need for server maintenance. The AMCR will be restarted during the night, but in case of problems, the outage may continue on Thursday, January 23. We therefore ask for your patience and understanding; service will be restored as soon as possible.
