---
title: AMCR Outage and Migration
excerpt: Desktop AMCR has been discontinued; migration to the web version is in progress.
badge: Outage
---

The AMCR desktop client has been permanently shut down and the system is being migrated to the web version of the application. The AMCR and API will be unavailable for two to three weeks, and the AMCR Digital Archive may also experience outages. At the same time, the accompanying websites will also be migrated to the new version, i.e., this AMCR home page, the help section, and the form used to report construction or other projects carried out in areas with archaeological finds. During the downtime, you can already familiarize yourself with the new application at <https://amcr-tr.aiscr.cz/> (your user account created before June 3, 2024, has been transferred to this version, or you can register as a new user). A recording of the online training for employees of authorized organizations can be found on our YouTube channel or at this link: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EGKT8n2zpeo>.
