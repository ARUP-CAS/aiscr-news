---
title: Digital Archive Outage
excerpt: The Digital Archive will be unavailable on 2 August 2024.
badge: Outage
---

In connection with the ongoing transition to the web version of AMCR and the related technical modifications, the Digital Archive will be shut down on Friday, August 2, 2024. The shutdown will last several days, and the Digital Archive will be relaunched next week. The new web version of the AMCR application will also be launched at the beginning of next week. We will keep you informed about the launch and related news.
