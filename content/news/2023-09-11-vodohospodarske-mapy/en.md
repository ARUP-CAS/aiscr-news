The [downloads](https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/?page=documents) section now includes a set of [water management maps from 1955](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8335693) stored in the ARÚ Prague archive, which show planned (and implemented) modifications to selected watercourses.
