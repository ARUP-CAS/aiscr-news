## New versions of the AIS CR and AMCR websites are now live

We have launched new versions of the AIS CR (<https://www.aiscr.cz/>) and AMCR (<https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/>) websites.

The websites are more modern, clearer, and better adapted to mobile phones and tablets. They also bring several practical innovations:

- A uniform visual style across both websites.
- Better navigation and a clearer content structure.
- Updated information and more accurate service descriptions.
- Fully responsive design for comfortable viewing on all devices.
- English version of the website for foreign users.

The new version replaces the original websites. We welcome any feedback so that we can continue to fine-tune and develop the website!
