More than 5,000 photographs of hillforts by Jiří Marounek are now available in the [Digital Archive](http://digiarchiv.amapa.cz/).  
They are linked to corresponding sites and fully described with metadata.  
Plans from the publication *Encyclopedia of Hillforts in Bohemia* are also attached.  

The corpus now includes over 350 verified and precisely defined sites, which are also available in AMCR.
